# Reference Image 1: Butterfly Coloring Page

## Step 1: Analyze the Reference Image

### i) Objects/Subjects

* Large cartoon butterfly
* Three flowers in the foreground
* Sun
* Clouds
* Grass patches
* Hills and fields

### ii) Background

* Outdoor garden scenery
* Open sky with clouds
* Rolling hills and farmland

### iii) Lighting

* Flat lighting
* No shadows
* Coloring book appearance

### iv) Style

* Cartoon illustration
* Children's coloring book
* Black-and-white line art
* Vector drawing

### v) Color Combination

* Monochrome
* Black outlines on white background

### vi) Camera/View

* Front-facing view
* Butterfly centered
* Portrait orientation

---

## Step 2: Initial Prompt

**Prompt:**

```text id="qw4pqj"
A butterfly sitting on flowers in a garden with clouds and sun in the sky, coloring book style.
```

### Generated Output Analysis

The image contains a butterfly, flowers, and sky elements, but lacks detailed wing patterns, landscape elements, and professional coloring-book formatting.

---

## Step 3: Refined Prompt

**Prompt:**

```text id="wggdl0"
A children's coloring book page featuring a large smiling cartoon butterfly sitting on flowers in a garden. Large decorative butterfly wings with oval patterns, three large flowers in foreground, grass patches, rolling hills, farmland background, fluffy clouds, bright sun in the sky. Clean black outlines, no colors, white background, vector line art, coloring page design, highly detailed outlines, portrait layout, printable coloring book illustration.
```

---

## Step 4: Final Prompt

**Prompt:**

```text id="6d4m83"
Create a black-and-white children's coloring page. A cute smiling butterfly with large patterned wings sits among three big flowers in a garden. Include rolling hills, farmland sections, fluffy clouds, a shining sun, small grass patches, and decorative butterfly antennae. Use clean vector-style outlines, no shading, no grayscale, no colors, coloring-book line art, printable A4 page, centered composition, portrait orientation, highly detailed but child-friendly design.
```

### ii) Background

### iii) Lighting

### iv) Style

* Modern premium design

### v) Color Combination

* Black
* Dark navy blue
* Orange-gold highlights
* White typography

### vi) Camera/View

* Close-up product shot
* Floating smartwatch
* Vertical poster composition

---

## Step 2: Initial Prompt

**Prompt:**

```text id="6s7z6s"
A smartwatch advertisement poster with a dark background and product information.
```

### Generated Output Analysis

The generated image shows a smartwatch advertisement but lacks premium branding, lighting effects, realistic rendering, and professional poster composition.

---

## Step 3: Refined Prompt

**Prompt:**

```text id="lpd4bt"
A premium smartwatch launch poster featuring a floating modern smartwatch above a human hand. Dark black and navy gradient background, glowing orange highlights, futuristic product advertisement, clean typography, feature icons, dramatic studio lighting, realistic product rendering, professional marketing poster, vertical composition.
```

---

## Step 4: Final Prompt

**Prompt:**

```text id="mvvhyn"
Create a professional technology product launch poster. A futuristic smartwatch floats above an outstretched human hand. Dark navy blue and black gradient background with subtle orange-gold glow accents. High-quality 3D product rendering, realistic reflections, dramatic studio lighting, premium commercial advertising style. Include minimal feature icons around the product, modern typography placeholders, luxury technology branding aesthetic, ultra-detailed, photorealistic, vertical poster design, 4K quality, marketing campaign artwork.
```

# Result

The refined prompts generated outputs that were significantly closer to the reference images than the initial prompts. Adding details about objects, composition, style, lighting, colors, camera view, and layout improved the quality and accuracy of the generated images.

---

# Conclusion

Prompt engineering plays a crucial role in AI image generation. Initial prompts generally produce generic results, whereas refined prompts containing detailed visual descriptions create outputs that closely resemble the original reference images. By carefully specifying subjects, background, lighting, style, color combinations, composition, and image quality, AI tools can effectively reproduce complex images with high accuracy.
